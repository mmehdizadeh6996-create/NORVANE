Common questions.

Do you supply new or used turbines?

Available stock changes often. Tell us the model you need and we will reply with what is on offer, whether new, used or refurbished.

Can I buy a complete generator set?

Yes. A set can include the turbine, generator, gearbox, base frame, enclosure and auxiliaries, depending on the unit.

Can I see inspection records before I buy?

Available records, such as running hours, maintenance history and inspection reports, are shared before you commit. You are welcome to send your own engineer to inspect.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We arrange packing, export documents and freight to your site.

Can you help if I do not know which model I need?

Yes. Send your target output, fuel type and grid frequency, and we will suggest the models that fit.
