Mandate reference BE.2026

BANEJENERGY is appointed as Norvane's authorized representative for Asia.

Issued by Norvane Power Systems, Hamburg, Germany.

Norvane Power Systems confirms that BANEJENERGY, under the management of Masoud Mehdizadeh, holds the status of authorized representative for the marketing, promotion and initial customer support of Norvane's gas turbine and generator set offering across the Asia region.

Under this mandate, BANEJENERGY is authorized to:

  • Present Norvane's range of Siemens SGT-400, SGT-600, SGT-800 and GE Frame 5 and Frame 9 turbines to prospective customers in the region;
  • Receive, qualify and forward technical and commercial enquiries on Norvane's behalf;
  • Coordinate introductory meetings, site visits and early-stage discussions with prospective buyers.

All formal offers, pricing, contracts and final order confirmations remain the sole responsibility of Norvane Power Systems and are concluded only through direct written agreement with Norvane. This mandate does not transfer any authority to bind Norvane to a contract.
